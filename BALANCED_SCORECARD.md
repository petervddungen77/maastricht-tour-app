# Balanced Scorecard - Maastricht Tour App

## Overview
This balanced scorecard provides a strategic management framework for the Maastricht Tour App, tracking performance across four key perspectives aligned with our vision of becoming the premier digital tour platform for Maastricht.

---

## 1. Financial Perspective
**Objective:** Achieve sustainable revenue growth and profitability

### Strategic Goals
- Increase monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Optimize cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Improve profit margins

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Target | Current | Status |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | €50,000 | - | 🔴 |
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | <€15 | - | 🔴 |
| Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) | €25 | - | 🔴 |
| Profit Margin | 35% | - | 🔴 |
| Conversion Rate | 8% | - | 🔴 |

### Initiatives
- Implement tiered pricing model (Basic, Premium, Enterprise)
- Launch partnership program with local businesses
- Optimize marketing spend allocation
- Introduce in-app purchases for premium content
